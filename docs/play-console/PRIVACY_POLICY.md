## 1. Overview
Benne ("the App") is an offline-first educational application designed to teach the Kannada script (Varnamale), core vocabulary flashcards, and everyday spoken phrases. We respect your privacy and adhere strictly to data minimization principles.

## 2. Zero Personal Data Collection & No Logins
Benne requires **no account creation, no sign-in, and no login of any kind**. The App does **not** collect, sell, rent, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII), location data, contacts, photos, or behavioral analytics to external servers.

## 3. Local On-Device Storage (Settings Only)
All user information—including your optional local display name in Settings and your learning progress (XP score, daily streak count, completed curriculum units, mastered flashcards, bookmarked phrases, audio playback speed, and daily reminder schedule)—is stored exclusively on your device using Android private application storage (`MODE_PRIVATE` `SharedPreferences` in `sarala_kannada_prefs`).

You may permanently erase all local data at any time directly inside the App via:
**Settings (top-right gear icon) → Security & Local Privacy → Reset All Local Progress**

Uninstalling the App also permanently removes all locally stored information.

## 4. Device Permissions
- **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS` — Optional, Android 13+):** Requested only if you explicitly enable Daily Practice Reminders inside Settings. Used strictly to schedule local inexact study reminders on your device via Android `AlarmManager`. You may revoke this permission at any time in Android System Settings.
- **Zero Restricted Permissions:** Benne does not request Location, Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Call Log, SMS, Exact Alarm, or Broad Storage (`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` / `READ_MEDIA_*`) permissions.

## 5. Text-to-Speech (TTS)
Spoken Kannada audio playback uses the standard Android Text-to-Speech service (`android.intent.action.TTS_SERVICE`) configured on your device. Voice synthesis is handled directly by your device's operating system TTS engine.

## 6. Third-Party Services, SDKs & Advertisements
Benne contains **zero advertisements**, **zero ad-tracking SDKs**, and **zero third-party analytics trackers**.

## 7. Children's Privacy
Because Benne collects zero personal information and includes no ads, logins, external social features, or user-generated content sharing, it is safe for learners of all ages and compliant with COPPA and Google Play Families requirements.
