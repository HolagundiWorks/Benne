Privacy Policy — Benne

Effective Date: November 16, 2026  •  Package ID: com.benne.kannada
Developer Contact: vishwabhiram@gmail.com

1. Overview

Benne (“the App”) is an offline-first educational Android application designed to help learners master the Kannada script (Varnamale), vocabulary flashcards, and everyday spoken phrases. We adhere strictly to data minimization principles.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection & No Logins

Benne requires no account registration, no sign-in, and no login of any kind. The App does not collect, sell, rent, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII), location data, contacts, photos, or behavioral analytics to external servers.

3. Local On-Device Storage (Settings Only)

Any optional learner information—such as your local display name in Settings and your study progress (XP score, daily streak, completed chapters, mastered flashcards, bookmarked phrases, audio playback speed, and reminder schedule)—is stored exclusively on your device using Android private application storage (MODE_PRIVATE SharedPreferences).

You can permanently erase all local data at any time inside the App via Settings (gear icon) → Security & Local Privacy → Reset All Local Progress, or by uninstalling the App.

4. Device Permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS — Optional, Android 13+): Requested only if you turn on Daily Practice Reminders in Settings. Used solely to schedule local inexact study reminders on your device via Android AlarmManager. You can disable or revoke this permission anytime in Android Settings.
  • Zero Sensitive Permissions: Benne does not request Location, Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Call Logs, SMS, Exact Alarms, or Broad Storage permissions.

5. Text-to-Speech (TTS)

Spoken Kannada pronunciation uses the standard Android Text-to-Speech service configured on your device (android.intent.action.TTS_SERVICE). Audio synthesis is handled by your device’s operating system TTS engine.

6. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

Benne contains zero advertisements, zero ad-tracking SDKs, and zero third-party analytics SDKs.

7. Children’s Privacy

Because Benne collects zero personal information and includes no logins, ads, or external social features, it is safe for learners of all ages and complies with COPPA and Google Play Families requirements.

8. Contact Us

For privacy questions, support, or Google Play compliance inquiries, contact the developer at vishwabhiram@gmail.com.
